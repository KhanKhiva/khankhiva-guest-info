Fire Safety

Fire Safety

🔥

In Case of Fire

Call the hotel operator to report the fire alarm. If possible, take your room key and leave the building.

🚪

Evacuation Due to Fire Alarm

  1. Take your room key and carefully move towards the exit.
  2. Carefully touch the door and handle; if they are hot, proceed with extreme caution.
  3. If they are not hot, open the door carefully and be prepared to close it quickly.
  4. If the corridor is smoky, stay as close to the floor as possible.
  5. If possible, crawl on all fours.
Emergency Instructions

Emergency Instructions

⚠️

If You Cannot Leave the Room

  1. Turn off the air conditioner.
  2. Call the operator and report your location.
  3. Do not open or break windows.
  4. Place wet towels, sheets or curtains around the door and over the vents.
  5. If necessary, use a wastebasket to draw water from the sink.
  6. If smoke continues to enter, build a wet blanket shelter overhead.
  7. Do not open or break windows if smoke or flames are coming from lower floors.
🛡️

For Your Safety

We strongly encourage our guests to review the evacuation plan located on the inside of their room door in advance.
